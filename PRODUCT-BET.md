# PRODUCT-BET.md

## 1. What have I noticed?
In my town, people have real skills, plumbing, tutoring, hairdressing, appliance repair, sewing, but there's no easy way for someone who needs that skill to find them. People rely on word of mouth or asking around on WhatsApp groups, which means a lot of skilled people go unnoticed, and people needing help waste time searching.

## 2. Who experiences this problem or opportunity?
Two groups: (1) People in the town who have a skill, a trade, craft, or service they could offer, but no easy way to be found by people who need them. (2) Residents who need a specific skill done, a repair, a lesson, a service, and don't know who in their area can do it.

## 3. What do they do today?
They rely on word of mouth, asking neighbours or friends, or posting in informal WhatsApp/Facebook groups. Skilled people rarely advertise themselves formally, so most of this knowledge stays in people's heads or scattered chat history.

## 4. What is frustrating, expensive, invisible or slow about that?
It's slow and unreliable, you might ask five people before finding someone who knows a plumber. It's invisible, skilled people have no way to be seen beyond their own social circle, so their skill goes to waste. It can also be expensive, without local options people sometimes pay more to call someone from further away, or hire an unverified stranger online.

## 5. What could a very small application change?
A simple app where anyone can list a skill they offer, with a short description and category, and anyone else can browse or search skills by category in their town. That alone turns invisible local skills into something visible and findable.

## 6. What is the ONE action my application must make possible?
A person must be able to list their skill so others in the town can find it, and a person needing a skill must be able to browse and find someone who offers it.

## 7. How will I know somebody found it useful?
If people actually list their real skills, if the listings get browsed, and if someone contacts a skilled person because they found them through the app instead of word of mouth. Even a few real listings from real people in the town would show it's useful.
