# HH-002E: Red-team the RLS policies (read-only)

**OBJECTIVE:** Try to find ways a user could read or change data they should not, in public.skills.

**CONTEXT:** Local skills and requests app. Rules: anonymous visitors may read active listings but never phone_number. Signed-in users may read phone numbers of visible listings, insert only their own listings, and update only their own listings. There is no delete policy. Withdraw is status = 'withdrawn'. Migrations: supabase/migrations/20261004_create_skills.sql and 20261007_protect_phone_add_kind.sql.

**SCOPE:** Read-only. You may read supabase/migrations/, src/lib/skills.ts and SECURITY.md. Do not edit any file. Do not read .env.local. Do not connect to the database.

**CONSTRAINTS:** Do not suggest disabling RLS, using a service-role or secret key in the browser, or adding new services. Do not propose new features. Every finding must be about access control or data exposure.

**ACCEPTANCE:** For each finding give: the policy or line involved, the attack step by step, the risk (low/medium/high), and a proposed fix. Include at least: ownership transfer via update, reading withdrawn listings, phone number scraping by signed-in users, and missing input limits.

**EVIDENCE:** A report saved as reports/HH-002E.md, ordered by severity.

**STOP:** Stop after writing the report. I decide which fixes to apply.
